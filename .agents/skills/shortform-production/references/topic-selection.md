# Topic discovery and selection

Read when the user asks to find topics (for example, "carikan topik"), uses `--topic=auto`, or explicitly delegates the topic choice. Finding topics produces choices for review; choosing on the user's behalf requires explicit delegation. [Invocation](invocation.md) owns execution and delivery. A discussion about this option does not start production.

Discovery is lightweight editorial exploration. Use the board, supplied context, and production history to propose angles; reserve deep research, source comparison, and claim-by-claim verification for the selected topic. This applies to both human and delegated selection. If the request specifically depends on current facts or trends, check only what is needed to describe those facts accurately; otherwise frame candidates as questions or proposed angles awaiting research. A shortlist does not need a research dossier or verified evidence path for every candidate.

## Select before creating files

1. Resolve the board or supplied creative direction using the invocation contract and read the board's registered editorial and visual documents when selected. Identify the subject scope, audience, pillars, sourcing rules, and duration/format constraints. With neither a suitable board nor explicit creative direction, report the missing basis; account availability is not a basis for choosing a niche.
2. Read production topics in `TRACKER.json` and inspect relevant `EPISODE.md`, `SCRIPT.md`, and content packages for likely overlaps, including unfinished work. Compare the audience question, central claim, scenario, and takeaway, not just titles. Exclude repetitions; use a related topic only when its new angle gives a materially different takeaway and record that distinction. Account for existing production folders missing from the tracker before creating a conflicting slug.
3. Find 10 distinct, plausible topics. Compare board fit, usefulness to the intended audience, novelty against existing work, strength of the central question, and feasibility within the format. Judge editorial potential without researching each candidate's mechanism, studies, or supporting sources. Keep proposed explanations provisional; describe a topic as trending only with current evidence. If the constraints prevent finding 10 distinct candidates that fit, explain the shortfall instead of padding with repetitions.
4. Prepare all 10 topics as a numbered list or table. Give each a title and a short brief of 1–2 sentences covering its angle, central audience question, and intended takeaway to investigate. Source leads are optional when already available; source discovery is not required to complete the shortlist. Mark one recommended pick and explain its editorial fit for this board/audience compared with the alternatives. Present the full shortlist when the user is choosing or requests it; delegated selection follows [presentation and durable records](invocation.md#presentation-and-durable-records).
5. For discovery requests, wait for the user to choose or explicitly delegate the choice. A recommendation alone does not lock a topic. Keep a human-selection shortlist in the conversation; create production files and a tracker entry only after selection and a production request. With `--topic=auto` or an explicit equivalent such as "pilihkan satu lalu lanjut produksi", evaluate the same shortlist internally, summarize the selected topic and reason, and continue without a topic-picking pause. Automation options alone do not delegate topic selection. Follow [automation activation](invocation.md#activation) for delayed policies and conflicts: `after-topic` requires human topic acceptance; later activation points can coexist with delegated topic selection.
6. Once the topic is locked and production is requested, assign a unique slug using [storage](storage.md), create the production folder and tracker row, and record the decision in `EPISODE.md`: `topic_selection: user` or `auto`, selected topic/angle, board revision when available, concise candidate comparison, overlap check, questions for research, and any existing source leads. For delegated selection, also retain the full candidate shortlist and recommendation there. Research only the selected topic under [research and script](research.md), retaining URLs and qualifications in `SOURCES.md`; unselected candidates remain editorial ideas.

Discovery is complete when the lightweight shortlist and editorial recommendation are prepared and either presented for human selection or recorded for delegated selection. Selection is complete when the user chooses or the agent selects under explicit delegation one topic and angle that fit the direction and are meaningfully distinct from existing episodes. Record that choice when production begins. Topic discovery is intake, not a new root tracker stage; evidence sufficiency is resolved during research of the selected topic, before hook development and script drafting.

## Continue the selected run

- `--topic=auto` delegates only the topic decision; the automation policy separately controls later human review gates. Before delayed activation, retain the applicable human checkpoints. Topic acceptance activates `after-topic`; pending `after-hook`, `after-script`, and `after-storyboard` retain human hook selection unless separately delegated under the invocation contract. With active automation, select the hook under delegation and continue; otherwise present the 3 hook choices and wait before drafting.
- For external delivery, preflight identity and available integrations before costly synthesis/render work. Resolve target channels only from the requested account or an established board mapping. Missing required access or account identity remains a blocker under the invocation contract; automatic topic selection does not authorize guessing a destination.
- Once the production exists, freeze its topic for that run. On interruption, resume the recorded production and earliest unfinished stage rather than discovering another topic. A clearly new invocation creates at most one new episode; it does not launch a recurring publisher or process the whole shortlist.
- If full research later invalidates the selected explanation, narrow or revise that episode's angle within the board and record the change. Preserve work and explain a broader topic replacement; a verified claim is not inferred from a topic's initial feasibility.
