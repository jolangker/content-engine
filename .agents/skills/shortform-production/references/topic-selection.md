# Topic discovery and selection

Read when the user asks to find topics (for example, "carikan topik"), uses `--topic=auto`, or explicitly delegates the topic choice. Finding topics produces choices for review; choosing on the user's behalf requires explicit delegation. [Invocation](invocation.md) owns execution and delivery. A discussion about this option does not start production.

## Select before creating files

1. Resolve the board or supplied creative direction using the invocation contract and read the board's registered editorial and visual documents when selected. Identify the subject scope, audience, pillars, sourcing rules, and duration/format constraints. With neither a suitable board nor explicit creative direction, report the missing basis; account availability is not a basis for choosing a niche.
2. Read production topics in `TRACKER.json` and inspect relevant `EPISODE.md`, `SCRIPT.md`, and content packages for likely overlaps, including unfinished work. Compare the audience question, central claim, scenario, and takeaway, not just titles. Exclude repetitions; use a related topic only when its new angle gives a materially different takeaway and record that distinction. Account for existing production folders missing from the tracker before creating a conflicting slug.
3. Find 10 distinct, viable topics. Compare board fit, usefulness to the intended audience, novelty against existing work, evidence availability, and feasibility within the format. Use the project's research workflow for source discovery when factual/current evidence affects viability. Keep source-backed findings separate from hypotheses; describe a topic as trending only with current evidence. If the constraints prevent finding 10 supported candidates, explain the shortfall instead of padding with repetitions or unsupported claims.
4. Present all 10 topics as a numbered list or table. Give each a title and a short brief of 1–2 sentences covering its angle, central audience question, and intended takeaway. Include evidence leads or source links where factual viability depends on them. Mark one recommended pick and explain why it is strongest for this board/audience compared with the alternatives.
5. For discovery requests, wait for the user to choose or explicitly delegate the choice. A recommendation alone does not lock a topic. Keep the shortlist in the conversation; create production files and a tracker entry only after selection and a production request. With `--topic=auto` or an explicit equivalent such as "pilihkan satu lalu lanjut produksi", show the same shortlist and recommendation, record the delegated pick, and continue without a topic-picking pause. Automation options alone do not delegate topic selection. Follow [automation activation](invocation.md#activation) for delayed policies and conflicts: `after-topic` requires human topic acceptance; later activation points can coexist with delegated topic selection.
6. Once the topic is locked and production is requested, assign a unique slug using [storage](storage.md), create the production folder and tracker row, and record the decision in `EPISODE.md`: `topic_selection: user` or `auto`, selected topic/angle, board revision when available, concise candidate comparison, overlap check, and evidence leads. Continue full claim verification in [research and script](research.md), retaining URLs and qualifications in `SOURCES.md`.

Discovery is complete when the shortlist and recommendation are presented. Selection is complete when the user chooses or delegates one topic and angle that fit the direction, are meaningfully distinct from existing episodes, and have a credible evidence path where needed. Record that choice when production begins. Topic discovery is intake, not a new root tracker stage. Source discovery supports selection; it does not replace full research.

## Continue the selected run

- `--topic=auto` delegates only the topic decision; the automation policy separately controls later human review gates. Before delayed activation, retain the applicable human checkpoints. Topic acceptance activates `after-topic`; pending `after-hook`, `after-script`, and `after-storyboard` retain human hook selection unless separately delegated under the invocation contract. With active automation, select the hook under delegation and continue; otherwise present the 3 hook choices and wait before drafting.
- For external delivery, preflight identity and available integrations before costly synthesis/render work. Resolve target channels only from the requested account or an established board mapping. Missing required access or account identity remains a blocker under the invocation contract; automatic topic selection does not authorize guessing a destination.
- Once the production exists, freeze its topic for that run. On interruption, resume the recorded production and earliest unfinished stage rather than discovering another topic. A clearly new invocation creates at most one new episode; it does not launch a recurring publisher or process the whole shortlist.
- If full research later invalidates the selected explanation, narrow or revise that episode's angle within the board and record the change. Preserve work and explain a broader topic replacement; a verified claim is not inferred from a topic's initial feasibility.
