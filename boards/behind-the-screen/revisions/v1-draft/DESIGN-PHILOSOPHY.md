# Open Interfaces

The visible surface is a promise of a world underneath. An interface begins as an object the viewer recognizes, then opens just far enough to reveal the relationship that matters. Opaque planes, offset edges and carefully anchored paths give hidden operations a place. Depth represents a change of explanatory layer, not decorative dimensionality. Meticulously crafted alignment lets a viewer locate the same element before and after the opening.

The ground is pale lilac, the surface white, and the ink a deep aubergine. Their separation should feel like examining a familiar screen in daylight. Cobalt belongs to an active exchange, teal to an explained resolution, and amber to the input or question under examination. Painstaking attention to these functional roles matters more than colorful variety. Color always has a second cue in shape, wording, line pattern or position.

Questions use the broad, irregular confidence of Bricolage Grotesque Bold. Instrument Sans carries the quieter work of identifying a device, a destination or a state. Readable type is a material of the explanation: a few words placed beside the thing they identify. A display question can dominate an opening, but a later explanation should allow the relationship itself to become the hero. Master-level execution means reducing competing copy before reducing type size.

Movement follows responsibility. A request leaves a source, crosses a boundary, arrives at a destination and produces a response. A local event remains visibly local. A retained copy stays in its location when attention moves elsewhere. Every anchor is preserved with the care of a physical demonstration. Pauses are observation time, and the return to the familiar screen is earned by the cause the viewer has just followed.

The subtle thread is the distance between an effortless click and the work that makes it possible. The artwork should make that distance tangible without turning technology into magic. Independent compositions may open a device, compare two compartments or trace a route; shared material, precise path endings and restrained typography keep them recognizably related. The final polish comes from removing ambiguous crossings and refining scale, with meticulous care for the phone-sized view.

## Initial plan and critique

The selected subject is everyday digital behavior, with a non-specialist audience and one causal explanation per episode. The medium is original vector interface illustration with shallow, opaque depth. Palette, supported weights and measurable geometry live in frame.md.

Initial generic options—dark backgrounds, floating server cards and luminous abstract particles—would say little about who knows or stores what. They were rejected in favor of three subject-specific layouts:

```text
Local / remote exchange       Privacy boundary           Nearby delivery

      question                   question                   question
 device  <--- challenge       [ device | history ]        origin
         ---> proof                  |   ---> site           : cached copy
            website               boundary                edge ----> viewer
       spoken caption             spoken caption            spoken caption
```

The review sheet can display three samples side by side for comparison. This is not a requirement for three-card layouts inside the videos. No decorative texture, stock footage, generated imagery, preset visual identity or animation effect is needed for the current static package.
