## Proposed refinement

| Layer | v2 proposal | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial | Investigate a defined historical question; allow corroboration, contradiction, or insufficient evidence as outcomes. | Avoid making concealment or wrongdoing the required answer. |
| Content concept | Open a dossier, identify the source, read the relevant detail, test an interpretation, state the supported conclusion and its limit. | Give the viewer a usable result without manufacturing certainty. |
| Visual | Keep the physical-document character; enlarge evidence text, use fewer words, distinguish source material from editorial annotation. | Make evidence readable on a phone and make its provenance legible. |
| Demonstration copy | Use conspicuously labelled simulated material, without historical names, archival call numbers, or historical quantitative claims. | The v1 samples contain attributed quotations and figures without a verification record in the board folder. Their historical accuracy has not been adjudicated in this refinement. |

Proposed audience-facing promise: **“Menguji polemik sejarah dari jejak yang bisa ditelusuri.”**
