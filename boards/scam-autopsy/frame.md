# Forensic light table

## Frame

Treat each screen as evidence laid on a charcoal examination surface. The two visual planes carry different information. The luminous or paper insert belongs to the reconstruction. The surrounding desk holds the editor’s title, source and annotation. Archivo’s open lowercase carries conversation and compact heavy headlines. IBM Plex Mono gives dates and sender information an even rhythm. Use the fonts under [motion-preview/assets](motion-preview/assets), each with its OFL.

## Exhibits

Use one dominant artifact spanning at least 40% of the frame width. An SMS can fill a handset crop with the top speaker, a sender line and two or three short bubbles. A fictional email exposes sender and subject only. A checkout shows the nature of a charge on a torn slip. For QR phishing, show an interrupted fragment of a sticker with a lifted corner and missing modules. Draw the artifact from scratch for each case. All evidence is original vector work. Keep stock footage out of the cut.

A matte desk grid gives the eye a fixed reference as the specimen moves. A scalloped receipt edge, contact shadow and imperfect ring locate the reconstructed object in that space. Keep the dark pen on pale paper and the brighter coral on dark glass. Add a word label for the observation. In a loss diagram, light panels name the origin and apparent destination. A single traced line ends at the actual recipient. Elide payment details.

## Legibility

Set the main heading around 90–105 px on the 1080-wide canvas. Evidence to be read is at least 40 px, the citation at least 31 px. Set captions at 53 px with a 59 px line interval, one or two lines at a time, left aligned in the region above. Keep the right rail and lowermost frame clear of essential text. Hold the dated source through the scene and keep it distinct from the illustrative phone UI. The text, shape and position must each help locate the tell.

## Motion and audio

Start on evidence and move it within the first second. Let the handset settle, reveal new evidence, then draw the ring around the exact words as the relevant clause lands. Hold the ring still while the words are read. Change scale or cut from the payment claim to a money-flow diagram, then revisit the original evidence for the tell. Let a transfer travel in one direction. Allow a still hold for reading within the five-second motion limit.

Use a clear, close ElevenLabs voice at a conversational pace. Let the message arrival make a short phone buzz and use a muted ping on a new message. A quiet low tone under the loss reveal can suggest tension. Give the last line room to breathe. Keep effects below speech and omit a continuous music bed.
