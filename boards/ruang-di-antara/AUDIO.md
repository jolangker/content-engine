# Ruang di Antara — sound direction

Implementation clarification: 2026-10-20. The user authorized this correction after investigating missing SFX. It develops the existing intimate narration, selective sound and intentional pauses; the approved visual and editorial direction remains v1.1. Previous direction/spec files are preserved in `revisions/before-sound-design-2026-10-20/`.

Narration remains the focal sound. Use a restrained material vocabulary: a soft dry contact for a concrete action or agreement; a short, darkened air movement for a meaningful change in distance/direction; a rounded contact for connection. Select from real local sources, treating them gently when useful. Avoid bright notification tones, comic pops, cinematic impacts and repetitive transition whooshes that imply a different genre.

Reuse clarification, 2026-10-20: the user prefers reusing suitable existing SFX across productions. The frozen contact, air and connection variants are indexed in [assets/audio/sfx/manifest.json](assets/audio/sfx/manifest.json), with original-source provenance and credits. Use them when they fit the scene; they are a reusable vocabulary rather than an episode quota or a requirement to use all three. Copy selected files into the production, then decide timing and gain for that episode. The production skill's audio contract owns the reuse-first sourcing policy.

Attach a sound to what happens: a connection lands, a boundary is established, a return point becomes definite, or observed action is separated from an assumption. A new slide or subtitle alone is not a reason for an effect. Use sparse cues across the episode, with density chosen for the actual story rather than a numerical quota. Contrast can justify reusing a sound; automatic repetition cannot.

Place short cues at the visual event, away from a vulnerable phrase when possible. Set gain against the placed narration and assess the actual exported mix at ordinary playback level. The cue should be recognizable without stealing attention or blurring consonants. The same raw gain is not a house standard across different assets.

Keep reflective questions and the ending deliberately quiet. A continuous bed is optional: use a suitable, traceable instrumental when it serves the episode, or record why narration plus selective SFX better preserves its intimacy. Lack of a music candidate does not resolve the separate SFX decision.

Production cue planning, asset provenance, timing reconciliation and export verification follow the production skill's [audio contract](../../.agents/skills/shortform-production/references/audio.md). Store each episode's choices and exceptions there, rather than changing this board to fit one episode.
