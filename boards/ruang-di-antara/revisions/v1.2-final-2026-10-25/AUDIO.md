# Ruang di Antara — audio v1.2

Approved board direction, 2026-10-21. Narration remains the main sound. See DIRECTION.md for review state.

## Narration and pauses

Begin the relatable question conversationally, as if recalling a recognizable moment. Let the listener hear the question naturally; avoid an exaggerated presenter cadence. Become precise during explanation, direct when offering a line of speech and open during reflection. Place pauses at a shift in understanding or a dramatic line. Richer visual beats do not require rushed speech.

The production workflow owns synthesis. Use the root project `.env` default voice unless the user explicitly chooses an episode voice; explicitly record ElevenLabs provider, voice and `eleven_v4`, with delivery tags. This board revision generates no narration and does not change historical audio.

## Reusable material sounds

Reuse the board-root [SFX library](assets/audio/sfx/manifest.json): soft dry contact for an action or agreement, darkened short air for a meaningful change in distance/direction, rounded connection for a relation settling. Provenance remains in the accompanying CREDITS.md and source records. Prefer suitable existing sounds before resolving new assets through the production audio workflow. Copy selected media and its provenance into each production when used.

Plan start/movement/landing against the actual event. A multi-beat frame may use silence between one or two selected accents; it does not need an effect for each action. Related shots can continue one event across a cut. Avoid automatic transition sounds, bright notification tones, comic pops and cinematic impacts.

## Dramatic frames

The two charcoal frames should be supported by a change in delivery, a short pause, a withheld cue or a restrained material accent when it fits the event. Inversion does not automatically trigger a loud hit. A quieter dramatic line can carry the switch. Re-entering cream may coincide with a concrete action or change in understanding.

## Music and mix

BGM remains a separate episode decision. Use a traceable restrained instrumental only when it serves continuity and leaves consonants clear. Record why narration plus selective sound and silence is preferable if no bed is used. Set gains against the actual narration and assess the exported mix at ordinary listening level. No raw asset gain, cue count or LUFS value becomes a universal board rule.

Keep the final reflective hold quiet. Production planning, asset hashes, source licensing, timing and export verification follow the shortform-production audio contract. This static package does not demonstrate perceptual mix balance or provide an audio preview.
