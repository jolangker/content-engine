## Direct observations

- A recognizable environment remains available while the system changes.
- An incomplete attempt creates curiosity; a problem motivates a useful addition or change.
- The explanation repeatedly alternates obstacle and intervention.
- Parts, loads, channels, ground layers, and camera scale carry much of the explanation.
- Captions remain much shorter than an explanatory slide paragraph.
- The final whole rewards the viewer for following the intermediate changes.
- The first two references use spatial 3D views. The house reference uses a flatter illustrative section. The rendered images alone do not establish which software produced them.

## New creative proposals

The recurring yellow/teal builder, premature celebrations, travellers with luggage, Barlow typography, exact palette, 90-second default, and callback strategy are this board's implementation of the user's selected direction. They are not presented as features found in all three references. The user explicitly requested a comedy plot in the middle or ending, then accepted the proposed direction and duration before commissioning the board.

The new identity chooses softly edged miniature geometry and natural daylight. It preserves explanatory spatial continuity while allowing more legible theatrical acting. Matching the references' complete production quality still requires an episode-specific motion, narration, and sound test. These stills do not establish that match.

## Factual limits

No technical claim in the supplied videos has been independently verified for this board. In particular, numerical load/stopping-distance comparisons, geological statements, cooling depth/temperature claims, and universal `without electricity` wording cannot be inherited as evidence. Research each selected implementation through the production workflow. The new house route and railway are fictional illustrative geometry, not complete installations, simulations, or instructions.
