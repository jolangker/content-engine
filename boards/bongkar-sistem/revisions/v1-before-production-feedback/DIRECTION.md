## Purpose and selected concept

Explain why everyday systems need their different parts by letting the viewer build them through successive problems and solutions. The emotional path is curiosity, confident experimentation, comic interruption, and earned understanding.

Working name: **Bongkar Sistem**. Proposed audience-facing promise: **“Setiap bagian ada alasannya.”** Indonesian narration addresses the viewer as `kamu`. The intended audience is curious Indonesian general viewers; an exact demographic, handle, and publishing account have not been selected.

## Accepted through the conversation

- The user supplied three local video references and accepted developing this direction into a new board.
- Build a system progressively: attempt, obstacle, useful intervention, next complication, working whole.
- Use environmental dioramas, exposed sections, meaningful object motion, and camera moves that follow the explanation.
- Include situational visual comedy in the middle and/or ending. A recurring overconfident builder, premature celebration, and an ending callback were presented and accepted as the concept to develop.
- Default target: 90 seconds. Simpler topics may finish in 60–75 seconds. The presented planning allocation is 0–5 hook, 5–70 progressive explanation including a 3–5-second comic beat, 70–85 whole-system resolution, 85–90 callback. Fit these allocations to the actual story and audio.
- Requested endpoint: create the reusable board package. No episode or animated demonstration was requested.

## Proposed implementation in this package

- Warm daylight, natural outdoor colors, solid earth layers, readable material differences, and softly edged original 3D geometry.
- One consistent builder: yellow cap, teal overalls, cream sleeves, simple expressive poses. A situational character, not a narrator avatar that must appear continuously.
- Barlow for speech and series identity; IBM Plex Mono for restrained production metadata and occasional technical notation. See [frame.md](frame.md).
- Three independent 9:16 studies: a rail-world complication, a house section with schematic flow, and a mosquito-with-luggage comedy payoff. They exercise different storytelling jobs; they are not one episode sequence.
- A static four-pose character strip establishes the acting vocabulary. It does not demonstrate animated performance.
