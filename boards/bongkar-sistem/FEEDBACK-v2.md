## Evidence and limits

Consulted the test's EPISODE.md, final scene-plan.json, final storyboard, REVIEW.md, and sound_design.json. It has six major scenes and one rain ambience cue. Sampled intervals at 16.0–18.4, 30.0–32.4, and 46.0–48.4 seconds show little camera variation while captions/local states change. This is sampled evidence, not a complete motion audit. Subjective audio feedback comes from the user; the agent does not claim to have listened.

The old technical checks did not establish the requested creative experience. V2 treats noticeability, environment, pace, camera, and sound interaction as separate outcome criteria. Runtime/layout/ASR/loudness success cannot override those failures.

Retain the problem–solution engine, Indonesian speech, recurring overconfident builder, optional comedy/callback, and factual source boundaries. Do not invent components, historical chronology, or mechanical failure to satisfy a step quota.

The feedback authorizes board revision. It does not silently request regeneration of the test episode, approve new images, or authorize new synthesis. V1 is archived. The next review covers the v2 static package; temporal motion and audible SFX still need actual audiovisual evidence during production.
