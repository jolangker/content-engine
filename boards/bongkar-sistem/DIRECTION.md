# Bongkar Sistem - board v2

Status: `awaiting_review`. The revised v2 visual package is built and inspected. This is a user-directed board revision after a generated test. The editorial concept remains; visual, pacing, interaction, camera, and sound standards change substantially.

## Purpose and retained concept

Explain why a system needs its parts through successive problems and useful interventions. Audience-facing promise: **Setiap bagian ada alasannya.** Use conversational Indonesian, address `kamu`, and retain a sympathetic, prematurely confident builder for situational comedy and an optional ending callback. Handle, audience demographics, and publishing account remain unspecified.

## User-directed changes

The new direction is **semi-realistic technical visualization inside a continuous full-screen environment**. Action, visible consequence, camera attention, and audible cue form one explanatory event.

- Continuous populated setting beyond all frame edges. No isolated earth pedestal or blank color moat.
- Believable dimensions, adult proportions, modest bevels, textured materials, constructed architecture, and perspective cameras. Retain restrained yellow/teal identity. Humor comes from behavior.
- Every important intervention has a visible before, action/contact, and changed result. Enlarge the view until the change reads at phone size. Captions cannot replace the action.
- Keep **90 seconds as default**, typically 75-95 seconds, and **strictly above 60 seconds**. More meaningful steps produce the faster pace.
- Plan around **20-26 meaningful visual steps** for 90 seconds, most 2.5-4.5 seconds, adapted to researched complexity. A new angle alone is not another step.
- Author camera travel within shots: approach, tracking, angle/elevation change, diagnostic detail, and pull-back. Include useful zoom-in/out; cuts cannot be the sole camera language.
- Map every important audible interaction to an appropriate discrete SFX cue. Rain is ambience and does not satisfy interaction coverage. BGM is optional; preserve speech clarity.
