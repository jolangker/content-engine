## Reading copy

Dari beberapa obrolan, lo udah kebayang hidup bareng dia.

Dia inget pesanan kopi lo. Di kepala lo, itu jadi tanda: “Dia pasti bakal ngerti gue.”

Padahal, lo belum tahu gimana dia menghadapi perbedaan, menepati janji, atau menghormati batas.

Ada kedekatan yang belum kalian alami, tapi sudah lo isi sendiri.

Dalam pembahasan limerence, orang bisa terpaku pada seseorang, sangat ingin perasaannya dibalas, dan terus kepikiran meski ingin mengalihkan perhatian.

Ketidakpastian soal perasaan dia bisa ikut memenuhi pikiran.

Misalnya, lo baca ulang percakapan buat mencari tanda. Satu respons hangat bikin harapan membesar.

Saat responsnya berubah, lo sibuk mencari penjelasan supaya kemungkinan itu tetap ada.

Yang lo ulang di kepala: versi hubungan yang belum pernah kalian jalani.

Membayangkan masa depan saja belum cukup buat menyimpulkan limerence.

Perhatikan juga: seberapa sulit pikiran itu dialihkan, dan apa dampaknya ke keseharian lo?

Coba pisahkan: apa yang dia lakukan, dan apa yang lo harapkan dari tindakan itu.

“Dia inget kopi gue” itu kejadian. “Dia pasti cocok jadi pasangan gue” masih kesimpulan yang perlu diuji lewat saling mengenal.

Bagian mana dari kedekatan ini yang sudah kalian bangun berdua?

## Editorial and evidence checks

- The opening is an illustrative scenario, not a documented participant story and not a test for limerence. Keep the scenario visually distinct from the general concept explanation.
- N05 uses a descriptive formulation supported by the persistent, involuntary preoccupation and longing described in S1 and S2.
- N06–N08 use uncertainty and sign-seeking as possible features; they do not establish a universal causal cycle or another person's intentions.
- N10 explicitly prevents diagnosing from imagining a future alone. N11 is an open reflection, not diagnostic criteria, a validated screener, or a severity threshold.
- N12–N14 are an editorial reflection informed by the board's facts/interpretations pillar, not a validated treatment. The script does not claim that one exercise stops limerence.
- The final question concerns jointly experienced closeness; it does not assert that all unreciprocated feelings are limerence or that genuine love must already be reciprocal.
- “Dia” is a person the protagonist is getting to know, not an established partner. Do not fabricate a history of mutual commitment in the visuals.
- The metaphor should show factual actions as solid marks and imagined connections as clearly different outlines. Do not portray the real person as empty, deceptive, ideal, or malicious.
