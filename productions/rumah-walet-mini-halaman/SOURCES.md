# Sources & Claim Verification — Bikin Rumah Burung Walet Mini di Halaman

- **Production:** `rumah-walet-mini-halaman`
- **Board:** `budidaya-di-halaman` (Revision 10 — Editorial v9 / Visual v5)
- **Researched date:** 2026-11-28
- **Species & system scope:** White-nest swiftlet (*Aerodramus fuciphagus*, synonym *Collocalia fuciphaga*), wild insectivorous cave-dwelling birds attracted via habitat and audio calls into a compact masonry mini swiftlet house (*Rumah Burung Walet / RBW mini*) built in a backyard corner.

---

## Claim-to-source mapping

### C1 — Wild swiftlet colonization via habitat + audio calls (no direct stocking; location & time caveats)
- **Claim in episode:** Building a walled cavity with an upper entry hole (*lubang masuk burung / LMB*) and playing recorded swiftlet calls invites passing swiftlets to inspect the house, though colonization depends on habitat comfort, flight-path locality, and months of patient adaptation rather than immediate stocking.
- **Sources:**
  - Balai Besar Perpustakaan dan Literasi Pertanian (PUSTAKA), Kementerian Pertanian RI — *Info Teknologi: Beternak Burung Walet, Hasilkan Profit Besar* (17 Sep 2024): https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-beternak-burung-walet-hasilkan-profit-besar — Confirms swiftlets (*Collocalia fuciphaga*) are cave dwellers that forage freely for insects, are invited into purpose-built structures using recorded swiftlet calls, and depend on nearby insect food supply (rice fields, rivers) plus suitable temperature, humidity, and low light.
  - Dunia Walet — *Mengatur Posisi LMB* (30 Apr 2023): https://duniawalet.co.id/mengatur-posisi-lmb/ — Confirms the entry hole (LMB) needs unobstructed flying clearance and close access to the internal drop/turning area (*void*) so entering birds can maneuver safely.
- **Qualification:** Visual time-lapse in the video compresses months of bird observation into seconds. Colonization success varies strongly by region, insect abundance, and neighboring bird traffic; the episode makes no guarantee of rapid occupancy.

### C2 — Nesting room requires cave-like darkness (`< 1 lux`) via light baffle (*sekat cahaya / lar*)
- **Claim in episode:** Direct sunlight entering straight through the upper entry hole makes an unbaffled room too bright so swiftlets circle briefly and leave; adding an internal dark light-baffle partition (*sekat gelap*) shields the nesting chamber so swiftlets stay inside.
- **Sources:**
  - Markas Walet — *Tips Pencahayaan Rumah Burung Walet* (4 Dec 2024): https://markaswalet.com/article/tips-pencahayaan-rumah-burung-walet/ — Documents that swiftlets prefer nesting areas around `0 lux` (recommended `< 1 lux`), while the entry/void stays slightly brighter/dim so birds and fledglings navigate without colliding; dark interior walls and internal light-baffle partitions (*sekat*) around the drop zone block direct glare from the `~50 × 70 cm` LMB.
  - Theresita Herni Setiawan — *Studi Penelitian Pembangunan Rumah Walet*, Jurnal Teknik Sipil UAJY Vol. 12 No. 2 (2013): https://ojs.uajy.ac.id/index.php/jts/article/view/608/628 — Documents the partitioned RBW layout separating the front turning *roving room* from the sheltered *nesting room*.
- **Qualification:** Darkness in the 2D cutaway is rendered in readable deep slate-navy so the viewer can still clearly observe the birds, planks, and nests while understanding the contrast against bright daylight outside.

### C3 — Midday heat above `30 °C` stresses swiftlets; downward elbow PVC vent pipes release heat without leaking light
- **Claim in episode:** Midday tropical heat drives indoor temperatures above `30 °C`, causing discomfort and brittle nests; installing downward-angled elbow PVC ventilation pipes (*pipa ventilasi siku ke bawah*) vents hot air via natural convection while blocking direct light entry.
- **Sources:**
  - Markas Walet — *Biar Burung Walet Betah, Yuk Atur Suhu Rumahnya* (12 Dec 2024): https://markaswalet.com/id/article/biar-walet-betah-atur-suhu-rumahnya/ — Reports ideal RBW temperature around `26–31 °C` (daily optimum `26–28 °C`), noting temperatures above `30 °C` cause birds to exit without settling and cause nests to dry, shrink, and turn brittle; documents `4-inch` PVC pipe ventilation spaced below the nesting planks.
  - Markas Walet — *Rekomendasi Ventilasi RBW* (11 Nov 2024): https://markaswalet.com/id/article/rekomendasi-ventilasi-rbw/ — Explains stack-effect/pressure-driven airflow, cross ventilation, and explicitly recommends adding internal downward-facing elbow pipes (*pipa menggunakan elbow menghadap bawah*) plus anti-pest mesh screens so light cannot shine directly into the nesting chamber.
  - Yoshihara, Daru & Ardhani — *Pengaruh Suhu dan Kelembaban Terhadap Produksi Sarang Burung Walet di Kampung Engkuni Pasek*, Jurnal Peternakan Lingkungan Tropis, Universitas Mulawarman Vol. 4 No. 2 (2021): https://e-journals.unmul.ac.id/index.php/ptk/article/view/6056 — Field measurements in East Kalimantan showed RBW temperatures of `25–34 °C`, with the lowest-yielding building reaching `34 °C`.
- **Qualification:** Passive ventilation alone may still require roof insulation or evaporative cooling in extreme lowland heat; the episode isolates the elbow-pipe light-safe ventilation step in Round 2 before adding evaporative humidity in Round 3.

### C4 — Low humidity makes saliva nests brittle and peel; a shallow indoor floor water basin raises humidity toward cave levels (`75–90%`)
- **Claim in episode:** Dry indoor air makes early saliva nests dry out, lose adhesion, and detach from planks; building a shallow floor water basin (*bak air dangkal*) evaporates continuously to raise indoor humidity.
- **Sources:**
  - Markas Walet — *Dingin atau Lembap? Begini Kondisi yang Bikin Walet Betah* (18 Dec 2024): https://markaswalet.com/article/dingin-atau-lembap-bikin-walet-betah/ — Reports natural cave humidity of `80–90%` and RBW survey target `75–85%`; documents that low humidity causes nests to dry too fast, lose adhesion, and detach from planks, and recommends shallow indoor water pools/basins (*kolam atau wadah air*) positioned under vent airflow to raise evaporation steadily.
- **Qualification:** Excessive stagnant humidity (`>90%`) combined with uncleaned droppings risks mold and nitrite discoloration; balanced ventilation from Round 2 keeps the room humid without stagnation.

### C5 — Swiftlet claws slip on smooth planks; rough/grooved dry wood nesting planks (*papan sirip beralur*) give grip and saliva adhesion
- **Claim in episode:** Swiftlets cling vertically with four forward-curving claws and slip on smooth planed ceiling boards; switching to rough/grooved dry wood nesting planks (*papan sirip kayu beralur kasar*, e.g. dried meranti) lets claws grip securely and anchors saliva nests.
- **Sources:**
  - *A Study of a Low-Cost Edible-Nest Swiftlet Master House*, Journal of Sustainability Science and Management (JSSM), Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, Vol. 16 No. 4 (2021): https://jssm.umt.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/sites/51/2021/07/9-16.4.pdf — Describes *Aerodramus fuciphagus* foot morphology: four forward-pointing curved claws with no hind toe, clinging vertically to plank edges.
  - Markas Walet — *4 Kayu Terbaik untuk Sirip Gedung Walet* (8 Nov 2024): https://markaswalet.com/article/4-kayu-terbaik-sirip-gedung-walet/ — Recommends kiln/sun-dried wood such as meranti dressed with a rough/grooved surface rather than smooth/slippery planing so swiftlets can grip and anchor saliva nests cleanly.
  - Dunia Walet — *Pentingnya Pasang Fondasi Sarang* (25 Sep 2023): https://duniawalet.co.id/pentingnya-pasang-fondasi-sarang/ — Confirms smooth hard planks make clinging difficult, especially for young swiftlets.

### C6 — Loud/clashing calling audio agitates birds; dedicated quiet indoor nesting audio (*suara inap pelan*) along planks encourages roosting
- **Claim in episode:** Blasting loud calling audio inside makes swiftlets circle restlessly instead of settling; installing calm low-volume indoor nesting tweeters (*suara inap pelan*) along the wood planks creates a safe, calm colony atmosphere so birds roost and build nests.
- **Sources:**
  - Markas Walet — *Suara LMB untuk Pemula: Cukup Satu Suara Inap, Nggak Usah Ribet* (11 Nov 2024): https://markaswalet.com/article/rekomendasi-suara-lmb-pemula/ — Documents that over-driving/clashing audio degrades effectiveness and that low-frequency calm *suara inap* communicates a safe, settled nesting environment to new colonies.
  - Markas Walet — *Memilih Suara Panggil & Suara Ideal Bagi Burung Walet* (23 May 2023): https://markaswalet.com/id/article/memilih-suara-panggil-suara-ideal-bagi-burung-walet/ — Notes birds react aggressively or circle restlessly without roosting when audio volume/character disturbs them.

### C7 — Geckos climb walls to raid eggs and nests via the entry hole; a smooth overhanging zinc/metal anti-gecko frame (*bingkai seng licin*) blocks climbing
- **Claim in episode:** Geckos (*tokek*) climb exterior walls into the entry hole to prey on eggs, chicks, and nests; fastening a smooth zinc/sheet-metal barrier frame (*bingkai seng licin*) around the entry opening prevents geckos from gripping and crossing into the opening.
- **Sources:**
  - PUSTAKA BPPSDMP Kementerian Pertanian RI — *Info Teknologi: Mengenal dan Mencegah Hama Burung Walet* (23 Mar 2023): https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/mengenal-dan-mencegah-hama-burung-walet — Explicitly identifies geckos (*tokek*) as aggressive predators of eggs, chicks, and nests entering via the bird opening and vents, and recommends screening vents plus mounting a smooth zinc frame (*bingkai anti tokek menggunakan seng*, extending ~20 cm wider than the opening) instead of fiber that breaks down.
  - PT Rumah Burung Indonesia Jaya — *Hama di Rumah Burung Walet* (2 Dec 2025): https://rbi.co.id/id/2025/12/02/hama-di-rumah-burung-walet-ciri-ciri-dampak-dan-cara-penanganannya/ — Emphasizes physical exclusion (sealing gaps, non-chemical integrated pest management) to protect nest food safety and prevent colony abandonment.

### C8 — Ethical fledgling-safe harvesting (*panen penetasan*): wait until chicks fly out, harvest only empty nests at midday
- **Claim in episode:** Removing nests while eggs or chicks are present stresses the colony and risks abandonment; waiting until fledglings fly out and harvesting only empty complete nests calmly at midday keeps adult swiftlets returning peacefully.
- **Sources:**
  - JSSM UMT Vol. 16 No. 4 (2021): https://jssm.umt.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/sites/51/2021/07/9-16.4.pdf — Documents harvesting calmly between `13:00–15:00` (while adults are out foraging) with a clean stainless-steel trowel, selecting strictly complete nests with **no eggs or chicks**, initially every 3–4 months while the young colony builds up.
  - PUSTAKA BPPSDMP Kementerian Pertanian RI (17 Sep 2024): https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-beternak-burung-walet-hasilkan-profit-besar — Confirms careful harvest timing is essential so birds do not abandon the house.
  - Universitas Brawijaya Repository (2023): https://repository.ub.ac.id/id/eprint/203007/ — Distinguishes *panen rampasan*, *panen buang telur*, and *panen penetasan* (post-fledging harvest that preserves colony growth).

### C9 — First-harvest weight (`100 g` ≈ 13 complete nests) & dated national collector price (`Rp 8.310/g` raw mangkok, Aug 2026)
- **Claim in episode:** A modest first harvest from a small backyard RBW after a year+ of colony building yields roughly `100 g` (~13 complete dry white half-cup nests at `~7.5–7.8 g/nest`). At the dated Indonesian national farmer/collector benchmark for raw white mangkok nests (`Rp 8.310.000/kg` = `Rp 8.310/g`, August 2026), gross value is approximately `Rp 831.000` (spoken rounded: *"kira-kira seratus gram... sekitar delapan ribu tiga ratus per gram, jadi kurang lebih delapan ratus tiga puluh ribu"*).
- **Sources:**
  - JSSM UMT Vol. 16 No. 4 (2021): https://jssm.umt.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/sites/51/2021/07/9-16.4.pdf — Measured average dry nest weight `7.50 ± 0.22 g` overall and `7.77 ± 0.48 g` for half-cup cradle (*mangkok*) nests (`100 g` ≈ `13` nests).
  - Markas Walet — *Birdnest Price Update / Harga Sarang Walet Hari Ini* (updated **03 August 2026**, compiled from 12,000 Indonesian swiftlet farmers and exporters): https://www.markaswalet.id/harga-sarang-walet and https://markaswalet.com/birdnest-price-update/ — Reports national farmer/collector raw bird's nest benchmarks as of **03 Agu 2026**:
    - **Mangkok** (putih kapas, bulu ringan): **`Rp 8.310.000 / kg`** (`Rp 8.310 / g`) — **used for tag (`100 g × Rp8.310 ≈ Rp831.000`, `estimasi harga pengepul / Agu 2026`)**.
    - Oval: `Rp 7.391.000 / kg` (`Rp 7.391 / g`)
    - Sudut: `Rp 6.597.000 / kg` (`Rp 6.597 / g`)
    - Patahan: `Rp 4.401.000 / kg` (`Rp 4.401 / g`)
    - Note on market range: Mixed/lower-grade raw lots (*harga cong*) average around `Rp 6,8 jt/kg`, while heavy feathers, moisture, or yellowing reduce buying prices.
- **Qualifications:** Gross value estimate only (excludes construction, wood, audio gear, electricity, and maintenance costs). Actual colonization timeline, yield, and grade depend on regional swiftlet density, season, moisture, and nest cleanliness.
