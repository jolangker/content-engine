## Locked world rule

1. Activation: a person knowingly says something false to another person. Mistakes, silence, feelings, and unspoken thoughts do not trigger it.
2. Effect: one small cloud forms just above the speaker; rain falls within that cloud's footprint and follows its speaker. Bystanders outside the footprint stay dry.
3. Limits: shelter/umbrella can intercept drops, but does not dissolve the cloud. Other people's reassurance does not dissolve it.
4. Release: the speaker corrects the specific lie to its original listener. The cloud dissipates after the correction.
5. Cost: discomfort and public visibility, not an injury, punishment tutorial, or real weather claim.
6. Ending: father corrects his lie to the son; the son gives practical company. No convenient new exception.
