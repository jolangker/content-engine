# Hujan di Atas Ayah — script v1

Original Indonesian fiction. First-person participant narrator, brief comic opening and a restrained emotional ending. Estimated 110–130 seconds before synthesis; measured timing supersedes this estimate. Visual directions are not spoken. Captions use short spoken phrases, not the directions or delivery tags.

## Spoken script

Di kota ini, setiap kali orang bohong, hujan turun tepat di atas kepalanya.

Aku tahu itu. Tapi waktu pot kesayangan Ibu pecah, aku tetap bilang, “Bukan aku.”

Tiga detik kemudian, rambutku basah.

Aku lari ke bawah atap. Awannya ikut. Aku buka payung. Hujannya cuma pindah ke payung.

Ibu menunggu di teras. Akhirnya aku bilang, “Aku yang mecahin, Bu. Tadi kena bolaku.”

Awannya langsung menipis. Ibu mengambil sapu. Aku mengambil pengki.

Sore itu Ayah pulang. Langit cerah. Ayah langsung duduk di teras.

Aku tanya, “Ayah capek?”

Ayah tersenyum. “Nggak. Ayah kuat.”

Sebuah awan muncul. Bahunya mulai basah.

Aku mengambil payung yang tadi kupakai. Ayah menggeleng. Katanya, “Kamu masuk dulu. Ayah sebentar lagi.”

Biasanya aku nurut. Kali ini, aku duduk di sebelahnya.

Aku geser payungnya supaya kami berdua muat. Lalu kubilang, “Aku bisa nunggu.”

Ayah lama diam. Tangannya masih menggenggam tas kerja.

Pelan-pelan, tas itu ditaruh di lantai.

Akhirnya Ayah bilang, “Tadi Ayah bohong. Ayah capek. Banget.”

Hujannya berhenti.

Aku menutup payung. Ayah menyandarkan kepalanya sebentar ke kepalaku.

Besok potnya masih harus kami ganti. Tapi sore itu, aku nggak buru-buru mengajak Ayah masuk.

Kami duduk dulu.

## Visible sequence

Show a personal cloud and boy against a dry sunny neighborhood; pot fragments and ball provide the lie's evidence. Alternate reaction, hand/prop inserts and wider spatial views. Roof and umbrella intercept rain without removing its cloud. Mother hears the correction and offers broom; boy takes dustpan. Father arrives with work bag and a separate cloud. Boy's question and father's denial raise the rain intensity, without adding a second cloud. He initially declines umbrella/company. Boy sits, shifts an already-open umbrella over both actors and waits. A hand releases the bag before the honest line. Cloud and rain dissolve only after the correction. Close on quiet contact and a longer two-shot; don't end on a moral card or a cliffhanger.

## Delegated script review

Hook promise fulfilled. One rule, one linked household story, explicit listener continuity, no hidden exception. A father can be tired and strong simultaneously; the knowingly false claim here is specifically “nggak [capek]”, not “Ayah kuat”. Repaired draft v1 before synthesis: the father arrives dry and the cloud first forms during his explicit denial, avoiding an offscreen lie with an unknown listener.
