# Sources & Evidence — Minapadi Nila & Padi di Halaman

- **Production:** `minapadi-nila-di-halaman`
- **Board:** `budidaya-di-halaman` (`editorial v9 / visual v5`)
- **Research date:** `2026-11-28`
- **Species & setup scope:** Integrated backyard *minapadi* (*Oreochromis niloticus* / ikan nila + *Oryza sativa* / padi sawah) in a yard-scale framed tarp mini-paddy (`~3 × 4 m` to `~3 × 5 m`, ~12–15 m² total footprint) with a raised central muddy rice bed (*pelataran*, 5–10 cm shallow flood) surrounded by a deeper perimeter fish trench (*caren*, ~40–50 cm wide, ~50 cm deep). Visual cross-sections and multi-month growth transitions are illustrative time-compressed cutaways grounded in documented Indonesian minapadi engineering and husbandry rules.

## Claim-to-source mapping

| Claim ID | Substantive factual claim in episode | Supporting source(s) | Key evidence & qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | Releasing Nile tilapia directly onto newly transplanted rice seedlings (0 HST) lets fish disturb mud and uproot weak roots; fish should be held in a deeper perimeter trench (*caren*, 40–60 cm deep) for 10–15 days until rice roots anchor before ranging into the paddy bed. | **Pustaka BPPSDMP Kementerian Pertanian RI** — *Info Teknologi: Keunggulan Budidaya Mina Padi* (`https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-keunggulan-budidaya-mina-padi`); **KKP RI** — *Pedoman Teknis Minapadi* (`https://jdih.kkp.go.id/bahanrapat/bahanrapat_09082019140043.pdf`) | Both Kementan and KKP specify creating a deeper perimeter trench (*caren*, 40–100 cm wide, 40–60 cm deep) around the central rice bed (5–15 cm water depth) and either waiting 10–15 HST after transplanting or holding fingerlings inside the *caren* while rice roots establish. |
| **C2** | Shallow paddy water heats rapidly at midday and stresses tilapia (ideal temperature 25–30 °C); placing partial shade net (*paranet*) over the *caren* edge keeps a cool refuge for fish while leaving the central rice canopy in direct sunlight. | **KKP RI** (`https://jdih.kkp.go.id/bahanrapat/bahanrapat_09082019140043.pdf`); **Pustaka BPPSDMP Kementan** (`https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-keunggulan-budidaya-mina-padi`) | Tilapia thrive at 25–30 °C; *caren* serves as thermal shelter during midday heat. Shading only the *caren* perimeter avoids depriving rice of its required 5–6+ hours of full sunlight. |
| **C3** | Heavy rain raises water level to the bund/tarp rim and risks washing tilapia out unless an overflow pipe (*pelimpah*) fitted with fine mesh screen (*kasa/saringan*) is installed at the target water line. | **KKP RI** (`https://jdih.kkp.go.id/bahanrapat/bahanrapat_09082019140043.pdf`); **Pustaka BPPSDMP Kementan** (`https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-keunggulan-budidaya-mina-padi`) | Standard minapadi technical requirements mandate screened inlet/outlet overflow pipes (*saringan kawat/kasa*) so rainwater drains safely while fish remain contained. |
| **C4** | Golden apple snails (*keong mas*, *Pomacea canaliculata*) cut young rice stalks and lay bright pink egg clusters; synthetic chemical molluscicides/pesticides must be avoided in minapadi because they poison tilapia, so control uses bamboo trap stakes (*ajir*) and manual picking. | **Pustaka BPPSDMP Kementan** (`https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-keunggulan-budidaya-mina-padi`); **Kementan PHT / Dinas Pertanian** (documented in search synthesis across `pertanian.go.id` & `bulelengkab.go.id`) | Chemical pesticides are strictly avoided in minapadi to protect fish survival and food safety; manual collection of adults and pink egg masses on bamboo stakes (*ajir*) is the standard fish-safe PHT measure. |
| **C5** | In a closed backyard tarp mini-paddy without continuous irrigation flow, organic build-up can cloud *caren* water and lower early-morning dissolved oxygen, causing tilapia to gasp at the surface; fine-bubble aeration in the *caren* restores oxygen and water freshness. | **KKP RI** (`https://jdih.kkp.go.id/bahanrapat/bahanrapat_09082019140043.pdf`); backyard tarp-pond adaptation notes | Open rice-field minapadi relies on continuous irrigation exchange; in a static lined backyard plot, a gentle fine-bubble air stone in the *caren* substitutes for flowing water oxygen replenishment. |
| **C6** | Once rice panicles ripen and turn golden, flocks of sparrows (*burung pipit*) attack the grain; draped protective netting (*jaring pelindung*) over arched supports prevents bird grain loss without chemicals. | **BRMP DIY Kementerian Pertanian RI** — *BRMP DIY Dukung Pengendalian OPT Burung Pipit* (`https://diy.brmp.pertanian.go.id/berita/brmp-diy-dukung-pengendalian-opt-burung-pipit`) | Kementan recommends physical netting (*jaring penutup/JAPRI*) during the generative grain-filling and ripening stage to stop *burung pipit* damage cleanly. |
| **C7** | At harvest (~3 months), slowly draining water off the central rice bed through a bottom drain causes tilapia to congregate naturally inside the deep *caren* trench so they can be scooped easily without trampling the mature rice stalks. | **KKP RI** (`https://jdih.kkp.go.id/bahanrapat/bahanrapat_09082019140043.pdf`); **Pustaka BPPSDMP Kementan** (`https://pustaka.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/info-literasi/info-teknologi-keunggulan-budidaya-mina-padi`) | One of the core engineered functions of the *caren* (and *kobakan panen*) is passive fish concentration when the *pelataran* is gradually drained before rice and fish harvest. |
| **C8** | Realistic household harvest scale (~3 months): a ~12–15 m² backyard tarp minapadi with a 50 cm deep aerated perimeter *caren* (~2–2.5 m³ water volume, ~65–75 fingerlings 8–12 cm with supplemental pellets) yields roughly **15 kg** of consumption tilapia (~220–250 g/fish), while the ~10 m² central rice bed yields ~7–9 kg GKP (~5 kg milled rice for home kitchen use, consistent with 0.7–0.9 kg GKP/m² in KKP/FAO Sleman data). | **KKP / FAO Minapadi Sleman benchmark** (documented via `slemankab.go.id` & `pertanian.go.id`: 0.7–0.9 kg GKP/m², 3-month cycle); conservative backyard tarp density (~6–7 kg/m³ in aerated *caren*) | Qualification: Extensive open-field minapadi without intensive aeration yields ~0.15–0.20 kg fish/m²; a deep aerated backyard *caren* with regular pellet feeding supports ~15 kg over 3 months. Spoken script states *"sekitar lima belas kilo"* and *"padinya masuk dapur"* with zero profit or payback guarantee. |
| **C9** | Dated local market price for consumption Nile tilapia in Indonesia (November 2026): East Java consumer average **Rp33.783/kg** (updated **28 November 2026**, regional district range Rp30.000–Rp35.000/kg); East Java consumer medium/premium rice **Rp13.034–Rp15.091/kg** (27 November 2026, contextual reference only — rice goes to home kitchen). | **FishInfo Jatim — Dinas Kelautan dan Perikanan Provinsi Jawa Timur** (`https://fishinfojatim.net/`, *Perbandingan Harga Mingguan*, update **28 November 2026**: Nila = **Rp33.783/kg**, up +Rp564 / +1.70% from 21 Nov 2026 Rp33.219/kg); **SISKAPERBAPO Jawa Timur** (`https://siskaperbapo.jatimprov.go.id/`, update **27 November 2026**: Beras Medium Rp13.034/kg, Beras Premium Rp15.091/kg) | Episode rounds Rp33.783/kg to **Rp34.000/kg** for clear spoken/tag arithmetic (`15 kg × Rp34.000 ≈ Rp510.000`), explicitly marked **`estimasi harga pasar / Nov 2026`** with zero profit or net-income claim. |
