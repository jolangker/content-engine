## Limits and weakest read

Sound is measured, not heard: pronunciation, prosody, source timbre, perceived mix and comic timing are not subjectively certified. Temporal visual inspection consists of sequential sampled frames and seek/pixel checks, not an uninterrupted real-time moving playback. These limitations remain disclosed under automation; no human approval is claimed.

The weakest shot is the cleaning example: an explicit schematic screening tray explains fines separation, but it is less representative of an actual continuous ballast-cleaning machine than the surrounding track hardware. A detailed machine/conveyor model would improve a future revision. The general claim is cleaning/replacement, without asserting this tray is a field maintenance method. Character and environment are procedural technical visualization rather than photoreal imagery.
