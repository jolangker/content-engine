# Storyline decisions

Five seeds: load journey downwards; stones-only myth; incomplete builder railway; maintenance day; cross-section tour.

1. **Selected: the premature celebration.** One inhabited railway worksite; the builder lowers two rails and thinks the job is done. The camera follows each missing support function into a detail, then withdraws to the working whole. First two seconds: two recognizable steel rails meet bare ground and the builder raises a thumb. Beats: incomplete goal -> load problem -> sleepers/fastenings -> ballast/restraint -> water interruption -> drainage/maintenance -> whole -> chair callback. Look: board v2 daylight site, steel/concrete/stone and adult character, yellow/teal, Barlow. Sound: material Foley and narration, no BGM. Best continuity with board's explanatory engine.
2. **Not built: a load's journey.** A finished railway in a continuous descending camera dive, following one schematic load path to soil. First two seconds: a wheel approaches a steel rail. Beats: wheel -> rail -> sleeper -> stone -> earth -> reverse reveal. More elegant but less room for the board's character-driven interventions. Sound: rolling wheel, layered contact and narration.
3. **Not built: the night maintenance mystery.** An emptied track under work lights, with the meaning of removed dirty stone revealed in reverse. First two seconds: a scoop lifts dark ballast from under a sleeper. Beats: puzzling removal -> clogged pores -> unsupported sleeper -> clean/repack -> daylight train. Bold lighting alternative, but starting with maintenance delays the basic load-support answer. Look: directional work lamps, steel/amber, same typography. Sound: ballast cleaning and tamping rhythm under speech.
