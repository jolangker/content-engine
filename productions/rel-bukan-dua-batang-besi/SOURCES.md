## Claim-to-picture map

1. Two rails alone do not describe the support system: S1, S6. Opening is a fictional incomplete attempt, not an operating railway.
2. Load travels through rail, sleepers, ballast and supporting ground: S1, S6, S7. Orange arrows are explicitly labeled schematic; deformation/force illustration is not a numerical simulation.
3. Sleepers broaden support and support the rails; fastenings hold rail to sleeper: S1, S5, S6. No prescribed gauge or spacing.
4. Angular aggregate contacts, ballast beneath/between/beside sleepers and restraint: S2, S3, S6. Visible interlocking is qualitative geometry, not a measured friction model. No claim that all stones or track types behave identically.
5. Voids allow drainage, but water must leave support system through a valid route: S2, S4. Blue paths are labeled schematic, not measured flow or proof of hydraulic capacity.
6. Saturation can reduce support stiffness: S4. Puddle comedy belongs off track; it does not certify the chair or ballast as engineering instrumentation.
7. Fines/dirty ballast and cleaning: S3 plus S4 drainage limitations; avoid attributing all contamination to one cause.
8. Tamping restores support under sleepers/geometry: S1. Simplified tines illustrate insertion/squeezing, not a complete machine specification or maintenance procedure.
9. Whole-system answer applies to ballasted track; other track forms exist: S6. Closing says 'Pada jalur berbalas'.

## Editorial exclusions

No historical invention sequence, do-it-yourself installation advice, thermal-expansion/joint claim, train speed, load in tonnes, universal thickness, exact particle sizes, guaranteed safety/performance or claim of suitability for a particular Indonesian line. Full-scale setting, builder, trolley and chair are original explanatory fiction. Reconstruction order is pedagogical, not a construction sequence.
