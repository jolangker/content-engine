# Momen kecil, dinikmati lagi — script v1

Language: Indonesian (lo–gua). 204 spoken whitespace-delimited words. Hook 1 agent-selected before drafting. One concept; scenes 02–09 explain/discuss it. All dialogue and tea details are fictional illustrations. Estimated 90 seconds; replace estimates with native audio timing.

## Scene 01: Duduk di dapur

Cuma duduk berdua di dapur. Kenapa momen sekecil itu masih terasa hangat waktu lo ingat lagi?

Emphasis: Momen kecil. Palette: cream.
