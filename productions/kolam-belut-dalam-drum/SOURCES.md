# Sources and Claim Boundaries — Kolam Belut dalam Drum Bekas

Research scope: verify practical backyard Asian swamp eel (*Monopterus albus*) cultivation in a repurposed 200 L plastic drum in Indonesia, including drum preparation, mud fermentation hazards, temperature/light sensitivity, rain overflow and escape prevention, nocturnal feeding, water quality and drainage, predators, size-disparity cannibalism, conservative backyard harvest yield, and dated Indonesian market pricing for the income scene.

| **C1** | **Species & drum vessel:** *Monopterus albus* (*belut sawah*) is a scaleless, obligate air-breathing, burrowing nocturnal freshwater fish native to Indonesia. A 200 L plastic drum laid horizontally with its top side cut open (leaving ~5 cm rims at both ends, braced/wedged so it cannot roll) is an established small-yard rearing vessel; plastic avoids rust issues of metal drums. | FishBase: [Monopterus albus](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Monopterus-albus.html); IUCN GISD: [Monopterus albus](https://www.iucngisd.org/gisd/species.php?sc=446); Kalurahan Sidoagung, Sleman: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123); Pemdes Badamita, Banjarnegara: [BUDIDAYA BELUT DALAM DRUM](https://www.badamita-banjarnegara.desa.id/index.php/first/artikel/378) | Illustrated side cutaway shows the cylindrical plastic drum horizontally on braced chocks with mud substrate and shallow water. | **C2** | **Mud fermentation hazard:** Fresh organic mud mixtures (paddy mud, chopped dried straw/banana stem, compost/bokashi, and microbial starter such as EM4) cannot be stocked immediately. Microbial decomposition raises substrate temperature and releases fermentation gases; guides require ~14 days of fermentation (followed by 2–3 days of watering/airing and water replacement) until cool, dark brown, and earthy-smelling before stocking eels. | DPMD Provinsi Jawa Timur: [Beternak Belut Di Bak](https://dpmd.jatimprov.go.id/detail/beternak-belut-di-bak); Liputan6 (4 Feb 2026): [Cara Fermentasi Media Budidaya Belut](https://www.liputan6.com/hot/read/6271513/cara-fermentasi-media-budidaya-belut-dari-persiapan-bahan-sampai-penggunaan-di-kolam); Pemdes Badamita: [BUDIDAYA BELUT DALAM DRUM](https://www.badamita-banjarnegara.desa.id/index.php/first/artikel/378); Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123) | Fermentation duration varies (14–30 days depending on ambient temperature and mix ratio). | **C3** | **Temperature, light aversion, aquatic plants & shade:** Optimal culture water temperature is ~25–31 °C (DPMD Jatim targets 26–28 °C). Elevated temperature increases blood CO2 and reduces plasma/intracellular pH in air-breathing *M. albus*. Because *M. albus* is light-averse/shy, an exposed yard drum overheats and stresses eels; floating aquatic plants (water hyacinth / *eceng gondok* covering up to ~3/4 of the surface) plus overhead shade netting (*paranet/waring*) moderate heat and provide shelter. | DPMD Provinsi Jawa Timur: [Beternak Belut Di Bak](https://dpmd.jatimprov.go.id/detail/beternak-belut-di-bak); Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123); Journal of Experimental Biology (2018): [Acid–base regulation in M. albus](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/221/10/jeb172551/229/Acid-base-regulation-in-the-air-breathing-swamp); Trubus (15 Dec 2022): [Keunggulan Budidaya Belut...](https://trubus.id/keunggulan-budidaya-belut-di-kolam-bertingkat-dengan-air-bening/) | *M. albus* tolerates a wide acute survival range, but chronic midday heat and bright exposure in a small plastic vessel degrade comfort and feeding. Keep shade + water hyacinth framed as practical temperature and shelter moderation, not an exact thermostat. |
| **C4** | **Rain overflow & escape control:** Because *M. albus* is scaleless, slimy, and air-breathing, it can slip over wet edges and wriggle out when heavy rain fills a basin to the rim. Maintaining shallow water (~5–15 cm above the mud) with freeboard below the rim and installing a screened overflow pipe (*pipa pelimpah* with fine wire mesh/*kawat kasa* ~8 cm above normal water level) drains rainwater while retaining eels. | Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123); IUCN GISD & CABI Compendium: [Monopterus albus](https://www.cabidigitallibrary.org/doi/full/10.1079/cabicompendium.74114) (aquaculture escape pathways); Synbranchidae ecology summary: [Swamp eel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swamp_eel); Panduan Budidaya Belut Efektif | Qualification: Shafland et al. found limited evidence of long-distance overland dispersal in some Florida canal surveys; keep the claim narrow and physical—rising rainwater to the drum rim lets slippery air-breathing eels slip over the edge unless blocked by freeboard, screened overflow, and top mesh. |
| **C5** | **Nocturnal feeding behavior:** *Monopterus albus* is primarily nocturnal, actively foraging from late afternoon into night (after ~17:00) on earthworms, chopped snails, small fish, and supplemental pellets (~5% of stocked biomass). Daytime broadcast feeding in bright open basins often goes largely uneaten and decomposes. | IUCN GISD: [Monopterus albus](https://www.iucngisd.org/gisd/species.php?sc=446); DPMD Provinsi Jawa Timur: [Beternak Belut Di Bak](https://dpmd.jatimprov.go.id/detail/beternak-belut-di-bak); Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123); Dejeefish/Alamtani cultivation guide: [budidaya belut](https://dejeefish2.wordpress.com/2017/02/13/budidaya-belut/) | Qualification: Well-shaded ponds can sometimes train eels to feed earlier, so frame daytime rejection specifically around the bright open backyard drum before dusk feeding is adopted. |
| **C6** | **Water fouling, ammonia & bottom drain + trickle renewal:** Uneaten feed and metabolic waste accumulate in stagnant water, raising ammonia and turning water foul/reddish-brown, which can cause mortality. Fitting a low/bottom drain outlet with screening, renewing water gently via a PVC trickle/splash pipe (without strong currents), and dosing probiotic decomposer (e.g., EM4) keep water fresh. | DPMD Provinsi Jawa Timur: [Beternak Belut Di Bak](https://dpmd.jatimprov.go.id/detail/beternak-belut-di-bak); Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123); Dejeefish/Alamtani: [budidaya belut](https://dejeefish2.wordpress.com/2017/02/13/budidaya-belut/) | Keep flow gentle (slow trickle/periodic partial drain, not rushing current) and note that mud itself is not stirred aggressively while eels inhabit burrows. |
| **C7** | **Backyard predators & removable mesh lid:** Uncovered yard containers are vulnerable to domestic/wild predators such as cats, poultry, waterfowl/birds, and small mammals. A fitted wire-mesh/screen cover (*tutup kawat kasa/jaring*) over the drum opening blocks predators while remaining removable for feeding and checks. | DPMD Provinsi Jawa Timur: [Beternak Belut Di Bak](https://dpmd.jatimprov.go.id/detail/beternak-belut-di-bak); Kalurahan Sidoagung: [Cara Ternak Belut Di Rumah](https://sidoagung.slemankab.go.id/first/artikel/123) | Illustrated with a removable framed mesh lid over the horizontal drum cutout (also serving as secondary escape protection). |
| **C8** | **Size disparity & cannibalism grading:** *M. albus* is carnivorous and exhibits cannibalism mortality in culture when individuals differ markedly in size or under suboptimal density/feeding. Uniform seed selection (10–12 cm) and periodic size sorting (*sortir ukuran*, moving smaller individuals to a separate container/second drum) reduce losses. | MDPI *Antioxidants* 15(11):1402 (29 Oct 2026): [Optimizing Stocking Density for Indoor-Cultured Monopterus albus Fry](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/15/11/1402); Pemdes Badamita: [BUDIDAYA BELUT DALAM DRUM](https://www.badamita-banjarnegara.desa.id/index.php/first/artikel/378); Panduan Budidaya Belut Efektif | Qualification: DPMD Jatim notes cannibalism is lower during early grow-out when feed is ample and increases with age/size spread. Frame grading specifically around unequal growth after ~2 months rather than claiming constant cannibalism from day one. |
| **C9** | **Stocking, grow-out period, conservative harvest yield & dated Indonesian market price:** Standard 200 L drum stocking is ~2 kg of uniform 10–12 cm juveniles (~160–200 eels). ## Creative vs. Evidence Boundaries

- **Creative illustration conventions:** The side-cutaway horizontal blue/teal plastic drum, visible mud burrows, rising fermentation gas bubbles, day/night/rain transitions, and time compression (14-day fermentation, 2-month grading, 3–4 month grow-out) are schematic visual storytelling devices anchored to the board's Visual v5 cutaway grammar, not literal real-time photography or lab assays.
- **No unverified promises:** Do not claim guaranteed survival rate, exact feed conversion ratio, net profit, or payback period. Do not introduce unsupported chemical additives (note: Sidoagung's older repost mentions MSG/"vetsin" as an appetite stimulant — omitted because no scientific aquaculture evidence supports it).
