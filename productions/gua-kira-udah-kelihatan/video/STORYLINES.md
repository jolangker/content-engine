# Storylines

Human Hook 2 is already fixed. Routine telling choices are delegated after that selection.

1. **Inside and outside one canceled plan ? selected.** Start with the quiet speaker and a crossed-out calendar. Alternate a close internal thought space with what the other participant can actually observe; cups and calendar return when the speaker names the feeling and context. Cream/charcoal/brick/sand illustrations, selective Newsreader, full captions, restrained material sounds, intentional pauses, no music.
2. **One sentence acquires context.** Follow a short answer as it gains a feeling and the reason it matters, through editable words. Not built: it would put more of the explanation into typography than this board requests.
3. **A shared table in one continuous take.** Keep both participants, two cups and one canceled event on one stage, reframing details. Not built: one persistent arrangement would offer less composition variety across sixteen meaningful shots.

Five seeds considered: internal/external viewpoint; a sentence gaining context; one table; a sequence of mistaken guesses; a signal-passing device. Guesses risk implying the partner's thoughts, and a signal device risks making the psychological metaphor look like literal evidence.
