## Factual claim map

| Claim ID | Episode claim & practical boundary | Supporting source | Verified evidence & qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | **Ayam kampung / KUB grow-out in an elevated slatted bamboo stilt coop (*kandang panggung*):** 20 birds in a ~2 × 1.5 m (3 m²) backyard stilt coop (~6.7 birds/m², within the 6–10 birds/m² grower guideline) reach market harvest size in ~70–90 days (~3 months) for improved local strains (*ayam KUB*). | [S1] BBPP Kupang – BPPSDMP Kementan: *Kelebihan dan Kekurangan Ternak Ayam KUB* (`https://bbppkupang.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/blog/kelebihan-dan-kekurangan-ternak-ayam-kub`); [S2] BRMP/BSIP Jatim Kementan (`https://jatim.brmp.pertanian.go.id/storage/assets/uploads/file/djHBQ7qBVb9FZYkRRXrWb0iD0h0dN1FeM0pV5jID.pdf`) | S1 documents *ayam KUB* harvest at **70–90 days** (faster than unimproved village chicken at ~5 months) with strong disease resistance. Grower stilt-coop density guideline is 6–10 birds/m²; keeping 20 birds in ~3 m² leaves comfortable ventilation and access. Starter chicks (0–4 weeks) require a separate heated brooder before entering an unheated slatted coop; the episode stocks post-brooder young chickens or focuses on the grow-out stilt coop. |
| **C2** | **Open floor feed tray scratched through bamboo slats → hanging feeder at back height:** Chickens naturally scratch (*mengais*) shallow open trays, wasting feed (up to 2–5 g/bird/day) and dropping it through slatted floors into droppings. Switching to a hanging feeder adjusted level with the chicken's back prevents scratching and cuts feed loss. | [S3] PT Medion: *Tips Memilih Tempat Ransum dan Air Minum* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/tips-memilih-tempat-ransum-dan-air-minum/`) | S3 notes spilled/scattered feed can reach **2–5 g per bird per day**, reducing nutrition intake and wasting feed cost; properly suspended feeders with height set level with the birds' backs and feed-guard dividers keep feed clean and contained. |
| **C3** | **Wet under-coop droppings & ammonia odor → dry rice husks + agricultural lime in the pit:** Nitrogenous waste (urea/uric acid) in damp droppings is broken down by ureolytic bacteria into ammonia gas ($NH_3$). At ≥20 ppm ammonia damages respiratory cilia (ciliostasis/deciliation), opening the door to CRD/coryza, and wet feces attract flies. Spreading dry rice husks and lime under the stilt pit absorbs moisture, suppresses ureolytic bacteria/ammonia, and cuts odor and flies. | [S4] PT Medion: *Kadar Amonia Tinggi dan Sistem Pertahanan Tubuh Ayam* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/kadar-amonia-terhadap-sistem-pertahanan-tubuh-primer/`); [S5] PT Medion: *Mengatasi Bau Amonia di Kandang Layer* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/mengatasi-bau-amonia-di-kandang-layer/`) | S4 explains ureolytic breakdown of urea/uric acid into $NH_3$, ciliostasis/deciliation at **20 ppm**, and respiratory vulnerability (CRD, coryza). S5 highlights elevated-coop under-pit management (periodic removal, keeping droppings dry, and suppressing flies whose saliva accelerates ammonia release). |
| **C4** | **Open water bowl fouled by feathers/droppings → hanging nipple drinker pipe:** Open water bowls become dirty quickly and act as a transmission route for respiratory diseases (coryza, CRD). Switching to a suspended nipple-drinker line supplied from a small clean tank keeps water hygienic and keeps the pit dry. | [S3] PT Medion: *Tips Memilih Tempat Ransum dan Air Minum* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/tips-memilih-tempat-ransum-dan-air-minum/`) | S3 states manual open drinkers act as a disease transmission medium when contaminated, whereas **nipple drinkers** keep water fresh and clean, prevent wet spills, and help suppress respiratory transmission (coryza, CRD complex, ILT, AI). Requires periodic pipe/nipple maintenance against biofilm. |
| **C5** | **Midday heat stress → slanted shade roof with upper cross-ventilation gap:** Chickens' comfort zone is 25–28 °C (60–70% RH). Above 28 °C chickens suffer heat stress (open-mouth panting and spread wings). Adding a slanted shade roof with an upper airflow gap exhausts hot air and cools the coop. | [S6] PT Medion: *Heat Stress pada Musim Kemarau* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/heat-stress-pada-musim-kemarau/`) | S6 defines chicken comfort zone at **25–28 °C** / 60–70% RH and heat stress above 28 °C; maximizing open-house airflow and shading relieves accumulated heat and lets birds settle calmly. |
| **C6** | **Wind-driven rain & cold night drafts → roll-down side tarp curtains (with upper vent gap):** Rain splash and cold night wind cause cold stress, elevated humidity, lowered immunity, and respiratory illness. Fitting roll-down side tarp curtains blocks rain and cold drafts while preserving an upper ~20 cm ventilation gap so fresh air circulates and ammonia escapes. | [S7] PT Medion: *Persiapan Kandang Menghadapi Musim Hujan* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/persiapan-kandang-menghadapi-musim-hujan/`) | S7 recommends flexible roll-up/down side plastic/tarp curtains closed on the windward side during heavy rain or cold nights while leaving ~20 cm upper ventilation so oxygen stays fresh and ammonia does not accumulate. |
| **C7** | **Night crowding & slipped toes on bare floor slats → round wooden roosting perches:** Chickens instinctively roost off the floor at night; without perches they crowd on bare slats where toes can slip between bamboo gaps and feathers soil. Adding round wooden perches (~3–5 cm diameter, elevated above floor slats) lets birds grip comfortably and sleep calmly. | [S8] Ditjen PKH Kementerian Pertanian: *Buku Panduan Pemeliharaan Ayam Bebas Sangkar (Cage-Free)* (`https://ditjenpkh.pertanian.go.id/storage/master/file/aONroQaT_BUKU_CageFree.pdf`) | S8 specifies natural nighttime roosting behavior, perch diameter (~3–5 cm, non-slip, splinter-free round wood/bamboo), ~40–60 cm height, and ~15–20 cm perch length per bird to reduce stress, crowding, feather pecking, and floor-contact issues. |
| **C8** | **Nighttime rats entering wall gaps → fine perimeter wire mesh (*kawat ram*):** Rats invade through open bamboo wall gaps at night, panicking chickens, stealing feed, and carrying poultry diseases. Wrapping walls and openings with tight wire mesh is core structural biosecurity. | [S9] PT Medion: *Kolaborasi Closed House dan Biosecurity* (`https://www.medion.co.id/info-medion/kolaborasi-closed-house-dan-biosecurity/`) | S9 outlines structural biosecurity (physical barriers isolating the flock from wild animals and rodents) combined with perimeter sanitation. |
| **C9** | **First-harvest gross value estimate (`estimasi harga pasar / Nov 2026`):** 20 market-ready *ayam kampung* at ~Rp70.000/bird ≈ Rp1.400.000 gross traditional-market reference value. | [S10] SISKAPERBAPO Pemerintah Provinsi Jawa Timur (`https://siskaperbapo.jatimprov.go.id/`, Update **2026-11-27**) | Directly observed on 2026-11-28 from the live SISKAPERBAPO Jatim table dated **2026-11-27**: row 10 **`Daging Ayam Kampung / ekor` = `Rp70.029` per bird** (East Java provincial traditional-market average). Rounded conservatively to **Rp70.000/ekor** for spoken clarity and tag arithmetic (`20 ekor × Rp70.000 ≈ Rp1.400.000`). **Important qualification:** This is a gross traditional-market reference figure per ready-to-cook bird, NOT net profit; it excludes chick/starter purchase, 3 months of feed, coop materials, vaccine/vitamins, and slaughtering/dressing costs, and live farm-gate collector (*pengepul*) prices are typically lower (often Rp35.000–Rp50.000/ekor depending on live weight and region). Labelled explicitly as `estimasi harga pasar / Nov 2026` with zero profit or payback claim. |
