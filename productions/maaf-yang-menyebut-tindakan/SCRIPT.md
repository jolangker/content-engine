# Maaf yang menyebut tindakan — script v1

183 spoken words. Hook 1 selected under delegated automation after source-body research and before drafting. Fictional ordinary lateness; the researched component does not guarantee forgiveness. Lines 2–9 explain/discuss the concept and evidence boundary before one connected application. Timings are estimates until native recording placement.
