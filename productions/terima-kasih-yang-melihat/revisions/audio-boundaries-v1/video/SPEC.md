# Director's specification

GOAL: Help viewers express one sincere, observed contribution in a thank-you, leaving room for the partner's response.

OUTPUT: HyperFrames; 1080x1920, 30 fps, approximately 90 seconds. Sixteen meaningful scenes; exactly two charcoal inversions at 8 and 10. Native narration speed; actual duration follows measured alignment plus explicit pauses and final hold.

LOOK: Approved board editorial v1.3 / visual-audio v1.2. Flat cream #F3EDE3, charcoal #302B28, brick #A34F3F and sand #D8C8B5. No shadows, gradients, textures or blur. Newsreader 76–82px headings, DM Sans 46px captions, frozen 26px chrome. Canonical board geometry takes precedence over generic vertical presets: content x88–936 / y230–1480, captions y1550–1740. Fourteen cream scenes, two charcoal with all reading layers inverted together.

OBJECTS: Two organic faceless participants with distinct silhouettes; plates with cutlery and inner rings, cooking pot with lid/handles/steam, grocery bag with handles and ingredients, cutting board with vegetables, clock with hands, doorway, table and speech outlines. The meal and cooking sequence are fictional. Paths show attention/contribution, not quantitative evidence.

MOTION: An opening situation is visible at scene start. Three semantic developments at roughly 14%, 43%, 73% of each scene; vary finite draws, 38–45px arrivals and 0.84→1 settling scale. Durations 0.18–0.9 seconds, power2.inOut draws, power3.out arrivals. No endless motion. Chrome and headings stay fixed. Final action resolves by approximately 85% of the closing shot and holds.

SHOTS: The ordered sixteen shot windows, focal actions, narration, reading windows, audio decisions, and handoffs are canonical in STORYBOARD.md and scene_schedule.json. After synthesis, timing.json supplies measured word/scene boundaries; estimated static storyboard remains preserved as the pre-audio plan.

BANNED: B-roll, phone mockups unrelated to attention, decorative glow/particles, stock photo substitution, supporting paragraphs over diagrams, labels as the sole change between compositions, symptom/diagnostic labels, gender/blame color coding, benefits-versus-effort moral ranking, invented study percentages, claims of guaranteed causality or lasting improvement. Cream/serif/numbered chrome are explicit approved board identity exceptions to generic HyperFrames defaults. Nothing may drift while being read.

QA: All 16 static landed poses before audio; complete 4x4 storyboard PNG; final review sheets at starts/midpoints/seams/phone size; four actual phases per scene; dark-scene caption/chrome contrast; caption coverage and clearance; native word alignment, audio presence and six material cues in decoded export; full export decode, duration, dimensions, hash and queue receipt. Perceptual listening unavailable must be disclosed separately from measurements.
