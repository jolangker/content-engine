# Sources: Kolam Lele di Halaman

Research completed on 2026-11-24 for standalone backyard catfish (*Clarias gariepinus* / lele dumbo–sangkuriang) rearing in a small yard tarp pond (~2 × 1 × 0.8 m, ~150–200 fingerlings) under board `budidaya-di-halaman` (Editorial v8 / Visual v4).

| **Tarp pond frame & liner puncture risk (Round 1)** | A tarp pond can be built above ground in a home yard using a bamboo, wood, or wire frame. The base must be flat and free of sharp stones, and the frame must have no sharp edges or protruding bamboo ends that can tear the tarp. Padding uneven ground with rice husks (*sekam padi*), banana leaf sheaths (*pelepah pisang*), or protective sacking/liner backing protects the tarp; split bamboo slats should face outward. New tarps should also be washed/soaked before stocking to remove chemical odor. | [Febriani & Witoko (2018), Politeknik Negeri Lampung](https://jurnal.polinela.ac.id/SEMTEKS/article/view/1221/845); [Dinas Pertanian, Ketahanan Pangan dan Perikanan Kab. | **Optimal temperature, shallow-water heat stress & shading (Round 2)** | Optimal rearing temperature for *Clarias gariepinus* (lele dumbo/sangkuriang) is **25–30 °C** (SNI 01-6484.5-2002; Sihotang 2018; Distankan Buleleng cites 20–28 °C). Shallow exposed ponds heat up quickly under direct sun, stressing or killing fish; fish hanging/surfacing (*menggantung/bergerombol*) is a documented warning sign. Shading (paranet/canopy or floating aquatic plants such as water hyacinth) and maintaining adequate depth buffer temperature spikes. | [SNI 01-6484.5-2002 cited in JOM Faperika UNRI](https://jom.unri.ac.id/index.php/JOMFAPERIKA/article/download/4209/4101); [Naturalis, Univ. | **Rain overflow & screened level control (Round 3)** | Heavy rain raises open-yard pond levels and can flood the pond rim unless controlled; ponds built in residential areas require level/overflow and drainage control. A screened overflow pipe (*pipa pelimpah berkasa*) installed below the rim lets excess rainwater exit while keeping catfish inside. Note: sudden rainwater can also lower water pH/temperature, and feeding during heavy rain is avoided. | [Febriani & Witoko (2018), Politeknik Negeri Lampung](https://jurnal.polinela.ac.id/SEMTEKS/article/view/1221/845); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng (2024)](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/58_cara-budidaya-ikan-lele-bagi-pemula-di-kolam-terpal) |
| **Wastewater handling & routing to yard plants (Round 4)** | Residential tarp ponds need planned effluent handling (catchment/soakaway/channel) rather than flooding paths or public drains directly. Catfish pond water contains leftover feed, feces, and fish excretions rich in nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium (K), making it usable as supplementary organic watering for yard vegetables/beds (while noting raw pond water alone does not meet commercial liquid-fertilizer N standards). | [Febriani & Witoko (2018), Politeknik Negeri Lampung](https://jurnal.polinela.ac.id/SEMTEKS/article/view/1221/845); [Agrifuture, Univ. | **Uneaten feed, bottom sludge, ammonia & bottom drainage (Round 5)** | Overfeeding leaves pellets to sink and decompose with feces at the bottom, increasing ammonia and degrading water quality; catfish show reduced appetite or hang near the surface. Feed is dosed around 3–5% body weight/day (2–3×/day), and floating leftovers remaining after ~10 minutes should be removed. Equipping the pond with a bottom drain/outlet (*saluran pembuangan*) and performing partial water exchange (30–50% morning/evening when water degrades, plus resting fish briefly) keeps ammonia and dissolved oxygen within tolerable limits. | [Dinas Perikanan Kab. Pamekasan (2020)](https://diskan.pamekasankab.go.id/berita/2020/03/02/mari-mengenal-teknik-budidaya-lele-tingkat-dasar/); [Jurnal Vokasi Ilmu Perikanan, Politani Kupang](https://jurnal.politanikoe.ac.id/index.php/jvip/article/download/1498/469); [Distankan Kab. | **Predator & escape protection (Round 6)** | Open backyard ponds are exposed to external animals (such as cats, birds, or other yard pests) and jumping fish. Extension guidance advises physical barriers/screens (*sekat pembatas/jaring penutup*) to keep foreign animals out of the pond. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng (2024)](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/58_cara-budidaya-ikan-lele-bagi-pemula-di-kolam-terpal) |
| **Size disparity, cannibalism & periodic grading (Round 7)** | *Clarias gariepinus* is naturally cannibalistic when size differences emerge in the same cohort. Periodic grading (*sortir ukuran*)—typically around days 15–20, 30–35, and 60–65—separates larger fish from smaller ones using a scoop and grading basin/net divider, after lowering water partially and fasting fish before/after (at least 8 hours after) to reduce stress and prevent cannibalism. | [Dinas Perikanan Kab. ## Illustration limits and qualifications

- **Schematic time compression:** Real catfish grow-out takes roughly 2.5–3 months (including initial water conditioning before stocking, periodic grading at days 15–20, 30–35, and 60–65, and staged water-depth increases). The animated build compresses these phases into sequential day/night and elapsed-time beats so each causal problem–improvement round stays legible inside the 90–112.5 s 1x window.
- **Visual simplification:** Water turbidity, heat waves, and fish counts are shown schematically at cutaway yard scale; real stocking (~150–200 fingerlings) is represented by a readable group of illustrated catfish.
- **Income disclaimer:** The income scene presents an illustrative **gross revenue estimate** (`15 kg × Rp23.000 = Rp345.000`) at documented East Java local market prices (Nov 2026). Actual yield depends on seed quality, survival rate, water management, and feeding; actual selling price varies by region and sale route (collectors/pengepul typically pay below retail market price), and feed/seed/material costs are not subtracted.
