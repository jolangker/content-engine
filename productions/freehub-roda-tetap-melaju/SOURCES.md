Primary topology: hub body 10 receives ratchet ring 20 with internal teeth 21; splined sprocket coupling 40 carries pawl driver 41, pawls 43 and spring 44. Do not inherit the patent's untested claims about eliminating damaging forces or superiority. Diagram uses two opposed pawls following this carrier/ring topology. Separate curved spring cues illustrate the outward bias described by B; they do not reconstruct A's annular coil spring path or exact dimensions.

Do not use B's claimed sound/price correlations, point counts, performance advice or brands as universal facts.
