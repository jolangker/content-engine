## S1 — Original capitalization studies

Gable, S. L., Reis, H. T., Impett, E. A., & Asher, E. R. (2004). What Do You Do When Things Go Right? The Intrapersonal and Interpersonal Benefits of Sharing Positive Events. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 87(2), 228–245.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.87.2.228
- APA/PsycInfo abstract retrieved through EBSCO: https://openurl.ebsco.com/fulltext/pdh:2004-16828-005?id=ebsco%3Apdh%3A2004-16828-005&crl=f&jrnl=19391315
- Four studies address sharing positive events. Sharing was associated with additional positive affect/well-being; perceived active and constructive responses enhanced benefits. Enthusiastic typical partner responses were associated with intimacy and relationship satisfaction.
- Scope: association does not demonstrate that a specific response guarantees closeness or diagnoses relationship quality. The episode makes no numerical effect-size, divorce-prediction or universal causal claim. Only the abstract of this paper was used.

## S2 — Original observed-couple study and response definitions

Gable, S. L., Gonzaga, G. C., & Strachman, A. (2006). Will You Be There for Me When Things Go Right? Supportive Responses to Positive Event Disclosures. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 91(5), 904–917.

- Primary paper PDF, retrieved in full: http://www.gruberpeplab.com/3131/6.1_Gable2006_WillYouBeThereForMeWhenThingsGoRight.pdf
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.91.5.904
- Opening and response-definition sections distinguish active–constructive, passive–constructive, active–destructive and passive–destructive responses. Active–constructive includes involvement and enthusiastic interest, including asking about the event. The study examines perceived responsiveness and relational outcomes in dating couples.
- Scope: heterosexual dating sample; context and individual/cultural differences constrain generalization. One brief response cannot establish a person's motives. The episode does not reproduce the paper's example or make breakup forecasts.
