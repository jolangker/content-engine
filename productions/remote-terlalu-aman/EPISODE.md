## Hook options, recorded before script drafting

1. **“Remote jatuh ke kolong sofa lagi? Kita ambil tanpa geser sofanya.”** Familiar inconvenience, visible objective, and a promise fulfilled through the retrieval route. Recommended and selected by the agent under active automation.
2. **“Cuma mau ambil remote. Kenapa sekarang rumah punya pusat sortir?”** Outcome-first curiosity; reveals the industrial escalation before it is earned.
3. **“Remote ini sebentar lagi jauh lebih aman daripada pemiliknya.”** Irony-first framing; weaker recognition of the opening physical problem.

Selected option 1 keeps the object and task immediately legible, saves the cutaway surprise and gives the final access failure a precise callback.

## Script direction and causal design

Script-writer preferences are resolved from this board: short narrative entertainment, simple direct Indonesian, restrained deadpan humor, no statistics, no greeting or CTA, measured technical delivery. Its longer YouTube templates are adapted to the one-minute format. The narrator is external and consistently addresses “kita”; one resident is the only actor. This is a fictional proposal, not DIY advice.

Approaches considered: a robotic gripper throughout (rejected: repetition); a magical teleporting remote (rejected: no readable path); mechanical suction → constraints → underground sorting → secure return (selected: every upgrade answers a visible failure).

Opening: remote beyond hand reach. Grabber pushes it deeper. Vacuum retrieves small objects but also grabs a cushion. A grate stops the cushion but blocks the remote. Enlarged intake admits remote and other household objects. Underground conveyor separates them and lifts the remote home. Secure transparent case prevents another loss. Its latch needs the enclosed remote, returning to inaccessible control.
