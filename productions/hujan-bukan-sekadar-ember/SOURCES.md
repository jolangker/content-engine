## Schematic and comedy

The model is a teaching reconstruction, not a dimensioned design. Blue water dots show direction, not measured velocity. The exposed first-flush chamber and tank are sectional views of otherwise opaque components. Simplified outdoor tap fills a watering can by gravity; no pressurized sprinkler or drip emitter is implied. Foundation support is represented by a stable pad; no load calculation or local code claim. Oversized mosquito, luggage, chair and premature celebration are original fictional comic actions. The joke does not establish engineering evidence.

All central claims are supported or explicitly narrowed. No exact collection volume, roof material safety, treatment efficacy, construction dimension, guaranteed savings or local regulatory permission is claimed.
