# Sources: Kolam Ikan Gurame di Halaman

Research completed on 2026-11-27 using `search_web` plus direct body verification of primary Indonesian government, university, and market-price pages. All claims below are scoped to household-scale giant gourami (*Osphronemus goramy*) grow-out in an above-ground circular wiremesh tarp pond (`D ≈ 2 m`, water depth `70–80 cm`) in Indonesia.

## Evidence map

| **C1** | **Circular wiremesh pond, conditioning & liner edge protection (Hook & Round 2)** | Above-ground circular tarp ponds built with a wiremesh frame are widely used in Indonesian backyard aquaculture for compact space efficiency and circular flow toward a central drain. Sharp wire ends or weld burrs on the metal frame can chafe or puncture the tarp under water pressure, so builders line the inner wiremesh wall/rim with protective gutter strip (*karpet talang*) and wash/condition new tarps (filling/resting water 2–3+ days) before full operation. | [Minapoli — Kolam Bulat Terpal Wiremesh](https://www.minapoli.com/mina/kolam-bulat-terpal-wiremesh); [BBPP Kupang, Kementan RI (15 Apr 2024)](https://bbppkupang.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/blog/langkah-langkah-menyiapkan-kolam-terpal-untuk-ikan-gurami); [RRI Cirebon / UMA](https://rri.co.id/cirebon/hobi/1280229/cara-mudah-budidaya-ikan-gurame-dalam-kolam-terpal) |
| **C2** | **Cold rainwater shock, temperature/pH drops & white fungus (*Saprolegnia*) (Round 1)** | Optimal water temperature for *Osphronemus goramy* is **24–30 °C** (Distankan Buleleng notes 25–28 °C; IPB Digitani notes 24–30 °C, pH 6–9, DO 4–9 mg/L). Because tarp ponds lack soil thermal buffering, direct cool rainwater triggers sudden temperature and pH drops that weaken gourami mucus/immunity and foster fungal infection (*jamur putih* / *Saprolegnia* cottony patches on fins/skin). Prevention uses an overhead clear UV/rain cover plus shade net (*paranet*), isolating infected fish for salt-water baths, and partial water replacement (~25% left to preserve chemistry). | [IPB Digitani (25 Jun 2024)](https://digitani.ipb.ac.id/bagaimana-cara-menanggulangi-jamur-pada-ikan-gurami/); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/trik-supaya-cepat-panen-gurami-12) |
| **C3** | **Taro-leaf (*daun talas*) supplemental feeding, wilting & leftover removal (Round 3)** | Giant gourami are herbivorous-leaning omnivores that accept leafy greens such as taro leaves (*daun talas*), *kangkung*, cassava, and papaya leaves alongside pellets (UGM thesis tested 25–75% taro leaf inclusion with pellets over 111 days). Fresh taro leaves contain irritating sap (calcium oxalate crystals) and should be wilted/aired first before feeding; uneaten leaves left floating or sinking rot, consuming dissolved oxygen and raising ammonia. Keeping leaves in a floating feeding ring and lifting leftovers preserves water clarity. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/trik-supaya-cepat-panen-gurami-12); [UGM Aquaculture Repository (2022)](https://etd.repository.ugm.ac.id/penelitian/detail/216755); [RRI Cirebon](https://rri.co.id/cirebon/hobi/1280229/cara-mudah-budidaya-ikan-gurame-dalam-kolam-terpal) |
| **C4** | **Rain overflow & screened water-level control (Round 4)** | Uncovered or partially sheltered backyard tarp ponds rise rapidly during heavy tropical rain; fitting a screened overflow standpipe/outlet below the rim discharges excess rainwater safely without letting fingerlings or adults wash out. Water depth for grow-out tarp ponds is typically maintained around **70–80 cm**. | [BBPP Kupang, Kementan RI (15 Apr 2024)](https://bbppkupang.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/blog/langkah-langkah-menyiapkan-kolam-terpal-untuk-ikan-gurami); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/trik-supaya-cepat-panen-gurami-12) |
| **C5** | **Calm water, central bottom drainage & gentle aeration (Round 5)** | Gourami thrive in calm/low-current habitat rather than fast turbulent flow, while organic waste settles at the center of a circular tarp pond. Distankan Buleleng recommends a **central bottom drain** (*saluran pembuangan di dasar tengah*) for periodic siphoning/sludge flushing and ~30% water exchange, maintaining DO (>3–4 mg/L; SNI minimum ≥2 mg/L, supplemented by gourami's labyrinth organ) via gentle fine-bubble aeration rather than strong jets. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/trik-supaya-cepat-panen-gurami-12); [IPB Digitani (25 Jun 2024)](https://digitani.ipb.ac.id/bagaimana-cara-menanggulangi-jamur-pada-ikan-gurami/) |
| **C6** | **Predators & jump prevention with top mesh net (Round 6)** | Backyard fish ponds face predation from stray cats and fish-eating birds (such as herons/egrets) as well as startled jumping; stretching a breathable mesh net cover (*jaring penutup*) over the circular rim blocks predators and prevents fish loss while keeping fallen leaves out. | [BBPP Kupang, Kementan RI — Hama Ikan Gurami](https://bbppkupang.bppsdmp.pertanian.go.id/blog/hama-yang-sering-mengganggu-ikan-gurami); Indonesian aquaculture extension guides |
| **C7** | **Feed competition, size hierarchy & periodic grading (Round 7)** | Because gourami grow at uneven rates, larger individuals dominate pellet feeding and suppress smaller fish (*size hierarchy* / territorial chasing). Periodic size grading (*sortir ukuran*) separates larger and smaller cohorts into distinct ponds/compartments so smaller fish catch up evenly. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/trik-supaya-cepat-panen-gurami-12); Indonesian grow-out grading guides |

## Illustration limits and qualifications

- **Schematic time compression:** Real giant gourami grow-out from fingerling to 450–500 g consumption size takes roughly 6–8 months (longer from fry). The episode compresses multi-month growth, fungal treatment/recovery, and feeding transitions into crisp sequential beats inside the 90–112.5 s 1x window.
- **Visual simplification:** Stock density (~100 fish in a 2 m circular pond) is illustrated by a clear, legible group of animated gourami in cutaway view, with subtle micro-bubbles, leaf-ring feeding, and central sludge swirl scaled for phone readability.
- **Income disclaimer:** The hanging scale scene states an illustrative **gross revenue estimate** (`40 kg × Rp45.000 ≈ Rp1.800.000`) based on East Java market averages updated 27 Nov 2026. Actual harvest weight, survival rate, grow-out duration, and farm-gate selling price vary by locality and feed management; input costs are not subtracted and no profit or payback is promised.
