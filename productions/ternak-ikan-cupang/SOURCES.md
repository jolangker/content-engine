# Sources — Ternak Ikan Cupang di Halaman

Research conducted on 2026-11-27 using the user-authorized default web search tool (`search_web`) plus direct body verification of primary Indonesian government/fisheries, academic, and supplier pages. All claims below are scoped to backyard ornamental *Betta splendens* (Halfmoon/Plakat standard grade) rearing in Indonesia.

## Claim-to-source mapping

| ID | Substantive factual claim | Supporting source(s) & URL | Key evidence & qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | *Betta splendens* are strongly territorial; placing an unconditioned pair directly together risks fin tearing and fighting. Pre-breeding conditioning uses a transparent divider, aged clean water, and dried *Terminalia catappa* (daun ketapang) leaves; the male builds a bubble nest, the female is removed after spawning so she does not eat the eggs, and the male tends the eggs until hatching (~36 h). | [RRI.co.id / KKP (28 Sep 2026)](https://rri.co.id/bengkalis/hobi/2768889/panduan-praktis-budidaya-ikan-cupang); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82); KKP / UBB / UNAIR fisheries extension summaries via search | RRI Bengkalis (28 Sep 2026, citing KKP research) documents 1:1 pair spawning in aged clean water, male bubble-nest building, immediate removal of the female post-spawning, and male egg care until hatching (~36 hours). Fisheries extension guides document transparent-divider conditioning (*perjodohan*) and dried ketapang leaves (*Terminalia catappa*) for mild acidification, antimicrobial action, and stress reduction. |
| **C2** | Direct outdoor rain and harsh sun disrupt shallow breeding/rearing boxes (cool rainwater / pH shock and physical splash break bubble nests; midday sun overheats shallow water and triggers algal bloom). Overhead clear rain cover and shade net (*paranet*) protect temperature, pH stability, and bubble-nest integrity. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82); Indonesian aquaculture extension summaries | Distankan Buleleng and Indonesian betta husbandry guides emphasize shading outdoor rearing tubs (~50–70% paranet) and keeping raw rainwater out of unbuffered fry/spawning tubs to avoid sudden temperature/pH drops and nest destruction. |
| **C3** | After ~3–4 days (once yolk sacs absorb and fry swim freely), the male is removed and fry cannot eat standard pellets because their mouth gape is microscopic; they require micro live feed (infusoria / rotifers / microworms / freshly hatched *Artemia* nauplii, followed by fine *Moina* water fleas). | [RRI.co.id / KKP (28 Sep 2026)](https://rri.co.id/bengkalis/hobi/2768889/panduan-praktis-budidaya-ikan-cupang); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82); [Perpustakaan KKP — Moina sp.](https://perpustakaan.kkp.go.id/knowledgerepository/index.php?p=show_detail&id=1074472) | RRI/KKP notes larvae require stage-appropriate live feed (rotifer, *Artemia* nauplii, *Moina*). Distankan Buleleng notes fry begin foraging at day 3–4 on fine live feed/microworms, progressing to *Artemia* at week 1 and larger live feeds thereafter. |
| **C4** | By ~4–5 weeks, growing juveniles outgrow small spawning boxes, waste accumulates quickly, and routine gentle bottom siphoning (~30% water change) is required using a fine-mesh-protected small hose so young fish are not sucked out. | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82) | Distankan Buleleng explicitly specifies transferring fry at ~5 weeks to a larger grow-out container/pond and performing regular bottom siphoning (*siphon*) with ~30% water changes to maintain water quality. |
| **C5** | Around 7–8 weeks (~2 months), labyrinth organs are active and male territorial fighting escalates, tearing fins; males must be sorted individually into soliter jars (*toples soliter*). | [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82) | Distankan Buleleng states that fighting instinct emerges from week 4 and sex sorting (separating males into individual containers) is performed at 7–8 weeks (~2 months) to prevent fin damage and fighting losses, with marketable sorting at 10–12 weeks (~3 months). |
| **C6** | Keeping transparent soliter jars directly side-by-side causes continuous flaring and chronic exhaustion; slipping opaque dividers (cardboard/paper) between jars blocks line-of-sight and reduces stress. Because *Betta splendens* are strong jumpers breathing surface air, jars need safe headroom and a breathable mesh lid. | UF/IFAS *Betta splendens* ornamental aquaculture extension & Indonesian breeder husbandry guides | University extension and hobbyist aquaculture guides document opaque dividers between adjacent betta jars to stop chronic flaring stress, and breathable lids/covers with headroom to prevent jumping losses. |
| **C7** | Conservative backyard first-harvest quantity & dated Indonesian wholesale price: one spawn yields ~500–700 eggs with typical controlled-rearing survival of ~23–28% to 2 months (~100–150+ juveniles), so **50 marketable bettas** at ~3 months (10–12 weeks) is a conservative backyard estimate. Dated Indonesian wholesale price for standard Grade C *Halfmoon* is **Rp10.000–Rp20.000/ekor** (using conservative lower bound **Rp10.000/ekor** → `50 ekor × Rp10.000 ≈ Rp500.000` gross estimate, not net profit). | [Super Koi Nusantara — Grosir Cupang (4 Sep 2026)](https://blog.superkoinusantara.com/jual-ikan-hias-grosir-cupang/); [Distankan Kab. Buleleng](https://distankan.bulelengkab.go.id/informasi/detail/artikel/memahami-teknik-pembesaran-ikan-cupang-dengan-benar-82); Indonesian aquaculture fecundity/SR literature (UNRAM / UMP / UB summaries) | Super Koi Nusantara (published **4 September 2026**, Ciater, Serpong, Tangerang Selatan) lists reseller/wholesale Grade C *Halfmoon* at **Rp10.000–Rp20.000/ekor** (Grade C *Crown Tail* Rp8.000–Rp18.000, Grade C *Plakat* Rp12.000–Rp25.000). **Qualification:** Wholesale/collector prices vary substantially by strain, fin quality, size, region, and bulk lot size (mass unsorted *partai* lots can sell lower at Rp2.000–Rp5.000/ekor, while Grade B/hobby fish sell higher at Rp20.000–Rp65.000/ekor). The episode states only a rounded gross estimate (`estimasi grosir / Sep 2026`) with no profit, payback, or income promise. |
